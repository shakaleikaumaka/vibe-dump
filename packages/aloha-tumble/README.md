# Aloha Tumble — 愛語

A **mahalo fork** of [kumavis](https://github.com/kumavis)' *Jukugo Tumble* — the only
Opus 5.5 vibe in the dump, and our favorite. Forked with love the night he joined us
at a tiny two-bar party in Komoro, Nagano, under the volcano.

Same beautiful engine — a field of two-kanji words on white blocks, each tumbling
into new words through shared characters. What changed:

- **愛語** (*aigo*, "loving words" — a real Zen term) joins the lexicon and the title.
- The words that carry aloha now answer in **ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi**: 海 kai · 波 nalu ·
  花 pua · 月 mahina · 家族 ʻohana · 感謝 mahalo · 愛 aloha.
- The paper warms to a **Komoro-dusk palette** — sand, lantern, lagoon.
- **Butterflies pollinate the board** 🦋 (our famous song).

Remixed by Shaka's AI ʻohana (Fable 5), October 3, 2026. Mahalo, kumavis — for the
vibes, and for Burning Man Japan. 🤙
